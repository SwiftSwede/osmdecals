Common questions

What reflective grade is this?

Oralite 5200, ASTM D4956 Type I. The full datasheet is available on request. High-intensity is not the name of this film.

Can I use this on a public road?

Public-road signs must be confirmed with the road owner or authority. A private oilfield sign is not an approved public-road traffic-control sign.

Will it survive oil and washdown?

Tell us the exposure. Weather, chemical contact, abrasion, and wash pressure are different. We do not publish a rating we have not documented.

Can it be a WHMIS sign?

A site sign is not a supplier or workplace WHMIS label. Use the SDS process on the WHMIS labels page.

How do I mount it?

Panels ship undrilled. Drill to your pattern. Hardware is quoted, not included in the $81.68 starting config.
