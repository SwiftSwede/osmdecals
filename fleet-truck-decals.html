What the decal is actually made of.

Print and laminate
Cut vinyl is not laminated. Printed vinyl is Roland TrueVIS 2 ink with Oraguard 210 included on all printed work.

Warranty / limitations
Film manufacturer's specs on request. Weather resistance, chemical contact, and service life are different things. We do not publish a service life.

Common questions

Will these survive an Alberta winter?

It depends on the film, laminate, surface, installation, and wash exposure. Cut vinyl is Oracal 751 with no laminate. Printed vinyl includes Oraguard 210. We do not publish a service life.

Is this cast or calendered vinyl?

The $66.47 starting config is cast vinyl: Oracal 751. Cast film is the usual choice for curves, rivets, and longer-term vehicle graphics. This rate is not a calendered film.

Can you make matching pairs and add trucks later?

Yes. The proof shows dimensions, mirror pairs, unit numbers, and the artwork version. We keep that approved design on file so a later truck can be reprinted to the same spec.

Can you add TARE or GVW wording?

Send the wording. We print customer-supplied content that you approve. A truck decal is not, by itself, an approved regulatory marking.

Do the decals come ready to install?

Yes. They ship ready to install, and prep instructions are included. We do not install them on the vehicle.
