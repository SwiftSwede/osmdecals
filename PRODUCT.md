# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Oilfield and industrial buyers in Alberta, Saskatchewan, and British Columbia who need WHMIS/OSHA labels, hard hat stickers, fleet vinyl, reflective signs, or coroplast signs. They are usually ordering for a crew, a lease, or a fleet, and they want a price and a ship date before they commit.

## Product Purpose

OSM Decals sells safety labels and marking made in-house in the MD of Bonnyville, Alberta. The site shows real rates, takes a quote or a free sample-kit request, and promises a detailed quote within 24 hours. Success is a buyer who can price a standard item and send a complete order request without calling first.

## Positioning

A division of 1st Impression Signs, making the labels and signs in the shop rather than reselling a third-party printer. Rates include assembly and UV lamination. Rush work is a stated 25% surcharge, not a hidden fee.

## Operating Context

Buyers compare minimums, then open a product-specific quote form or the general quote form. Forms post to Formspree. Phone and email stay available: (780) 207-3927 and sales@osmdecals.ca. Production to shipping is typically 5-10 days from the Bonnyville shop. Design time is $60.00/hr where it applies.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five priced products: 8.5 x 11 WHMIS labels ($6.41, min 25), 2.5 x 2.5 hard hat stickers ($1.86, min 45), 18 x 24 fleet decals ($66.47, min 4), 18 x 24 reflective polymetal signs ($81.68, min 4), 18 x 24 coroplast signs ($46.07, min 4).
- Static site: `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `css/tailwind.css`, `js/main.js`. No app framework.
- Quote and sample-kit modal ids, field names, and Formspree actions are production hooks. Do not rename them.
- SEO title, description, H1, and JSON-LD stay as published unless the owner asks for a copy change.
- Team named on the page: Charlene Rask, Trevor Rask, Kyle Rask.

## Brand Commitments

The name is OSM Decals. The wordmark is `assets/logos/logo.svg`. Headings are Montserrat. Body is Open Sans. The owner asked to keep this design: navy, safety orange, teal, and the existing section order. Do not replace the logo or invent a new palette.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public page should meet WCAG AA for text, form names, keyboard use, and dialog focus. Buyers include people ordering from a phone on a lease road, so tap targets and readable form text matter as much as desktop layout.
