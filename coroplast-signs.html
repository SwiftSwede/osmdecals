Common questions

Are coroplast signs weatherproof?

UV laminate is included. Wind, sun, and the mount still change how long a sign lasts outside. We do not publish a lifespan.

How long can they stay outdoors?

It depends on wind, sun, and how you mount it. These are temporary site signs. Bring them in during severe weather.

Are stakes included?

No. Stakes and grommets are extra. The $46.07 starting price is the laminated sign only.

Can you print both sides?

No. The standard sign is single-sided. A different layout is a separate quote.

What if I need a permanent sign?

Use reflective polymetal for a longer-term private-site sign. Coroplast is the temporary option.
