Supplier or workplace

Three starting points. One proof before we print.

Supplier labels come from the manufacturer's SDS. Workplace labels are for secondary containers. OSM prints the content you approve. Regulatory determination stays with the supplier or employer.

Common questions

Do you make bilingual labels?

No. Artwork is English. If you need French or bilingual labels, supply approved bilingual artwork and we will print what you approve.

Will the label survive chemicals?

It depends on the chemical, whether it splashes or sits on the label, and the surface. Tell us what it touches. We do not promise an exposure rating that is not documented.

Do you make supplier or workplace labels?

We can quote either after you tell us the use and send the SDS. Supplier labels come from the manufacturer's SDS. Workplace labels are for secondary containers. You confirm the regulated content.

Can you relabel a tote?

Send a photo. We need to know if the surface is flat, curved, or textured, and whether it is clean and dry, before we confirm the adhesive.
