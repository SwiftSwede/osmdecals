Common questions

Will they stick to my hard hat?

It depends on the shell, placement, surface, and adhesive. Send the make and model, or a photo, and we will flag compatibility questions.

Are they waterproof?

Weather exposure is not the same as chemical, abrasion, or washdown resistance. Tell us the exposure and we will confirm the option we can offer.

Can you make them reflective?

Oralite 5200 is available. We'll confirm for your application. Private-site visibility is not public-road authorization.

Are these WHMIS labels or PPE certified?

No. A branding or name sticker is not a WHMIS label and it is not PPE certification. Follow the helmet maker and your employer's rules.

Can you print employee names?

Yes. Send the roster. Each name is proofed, and you approve the spelling before we cut.
